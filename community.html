Community impact

Giving back, together.

AE uses technical knowledge, service, and collaboration to strengthen our school and the wider Arcadia community.

Why it matters

Sharing what we learn.

The skills developed through competition become tools for service. Members support younger students, collaborate with campus organizations, and participate in community projects.

Every partnership helps make engineering more visible, approachable, and useful.

01

STEM Outreach

Supporting middle-school STEM programs, library learning activities, and opportunities that introduce younger students to engineering.

02

School Partnerships

Sharing knowledge with VEX, Science Olympiad, EDD, Electrical Engineering Club, sustainability groups, and other student organizations.

03

Community Service

Participating in service and sustainability efforts connected with local museums, the Arboretum, Habitat for Humanity, gardens, and youth-led initiatives.

04

Mentorship

Creating a culture where returning members teach new members and technical experience is passed forward each season.

The competition

Every decision has a consequence.

The craft must perform under real limits on power, weight, reliability, maneuverability, and safety. Teams are evaluated on engineering, performance, design, presentation, and their understanding of the systems they built.

A lighter structure can increase speed. Better solar efficiency can extend endurance. Reliable electronics can determine whether the boat finishes at all.

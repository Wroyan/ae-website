Who joins AE?

Curiosity matters more than experience.

Our selection process looks for students who are dependable, collaborative, and willing to learn—not only those who already have technical skills.

Members work side by side throughout the school year, often meeting beyond scheduled workdays when a component needs testing or a deadline approaches.
